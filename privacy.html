2. In short

Mighty Theron needs no account. We run no servers for the game and do not collect personal data ourselves. Your progress and settings (progress, coins, equipment, language, volume) are stored only on your device and never leave it. They are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland; Google LLC, USA). This may involve your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and ad interaction data, used to serve ads, prevent fraud and measure advertising. Ads may be personalized or non-personalized depending on your consent.

Consent: where required by law (e.g. EEA, UK), we ask for your consent on first launch with a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform). You can withdraw or change your consent at any time through your device's ad settings (Android: Settings › Google › Ads) or by clearing the app data, which shows the form again. More information: policies.google.com/technologies/ads and policies.google.com/privacy.

Data may be transferred to the USA; Google participates in the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or uses standard contractual clauses.

4. “Remove ads” purchase (Google Play Billing)

The one-time in-app purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We receive no payment data, only the information that the purchase was made so we can unlock the feature.

5. Google Play

When you download the app, Google processes data on its own responsibility. See policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and the right to complain to a supervisory authority. Because we store no personal data about you ourselves, requests concerning advertising should be directed to Google.
